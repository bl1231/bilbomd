Inp Jiffy: the Preview step now draws the fixed and rigid bodies the way the job form and job page do, checks them the way BilboMD will, and offers both a const.inp and an openmm_const.yml download, with the file texts in collapsed sections. Step 2 calls the bodies fixed, rigid and flexible, and step 1 shows the example as both a const.inp and an openmm_const.yml. The Inp Jiffy and the PAE Jiffy each get a "Use in a new Classic job" button, which opens a new BilboMD Classic job with the structure and constraints already picked. The Inp Jiffy now ends on its Preview step: Finish and the empty last screen are gone, a Start Over button begins a new file, and the step shows as completed once a file is downloaded. A PDB file with no atom records or without chain IDs now keeps Next off (before, the error could clear and let it through), and going Back to step 1 shows the files already picked. The PAE Jiffy now shows its result the same way as the Inp Jiffy: the bodies checked against the model and drawn, the hand-off, and both a const.inp and an openmm_const.yml download. Its instructions start open and no longer tie the result to CHARMM, and up to 20 rigid bodies are allowed (it refused exactly 20 before).
