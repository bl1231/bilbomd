Owners and public results links now share one job page. It has a summary header, a progress card with a compact step strip (status icons with tooltips, the running step highlighted, per-step durations and a "Show details" list), a Job Failed panel with the error message and job UUID, and the analysis, Molstar and results sections. Input files and parameters move to a collapsed "Inputs & parameters" section at the bottom. Owners can now also resubmit or delete a failed job from its page. The in-page Register prompts are gone, and the MongoDB ID is no longer shown.
